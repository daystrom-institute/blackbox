# Historical whiteboard evidence

Whiteboards are historical records; creation, posting, voting, transitions and workflow signals are retired. Discover whiteboard refs through graph retrieval and use bbox_inspect_entity(entity_ref="whiteboard:<id>", property="body") for stored public evidence. Follow property pagination for long histories.

Inspection grants no board role. Blind posts stay hidden; annotations and votes retain their phase visibility. Retirement does not advance phases or confer facilitator authority. Both legacy root records and archive records are readable. Project ownership survives for rename and retirement administration. Compose new deliberation in the caller with bro_broadcast and bro_when_all, recording useful conclusions in an authorized thread.
