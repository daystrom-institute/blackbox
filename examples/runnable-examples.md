# Examples

[Graph exercise](graph-live-exercise.md) exercises corpus retrieval and evidence
bundling. [Session searcher](agents/session-searcher.md) is a focused retrieval
role. The `skills/` prompts illustrate caller-owned orchestration with bro
execution and resume; adapt model selection to `bro_providers`.

Workflow, atom, cron, Slack and reaction application examples are retired.
Their earlier revisions remain in Git history; no current installer activates
them. Optional brofiles, simple agents and packets live in
[system defaults](../system-defaults/system-defaults.md).
